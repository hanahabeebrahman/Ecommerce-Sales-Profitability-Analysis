# E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to understand revenue, profitability, product category performance, discount effectiveness, customer segments, and business trends.

The goal is to identify actionable business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## Objectives

* Analyze total revenue and profit.
* Compare profitability across product categories.
* Evaluate the relationship between discounts and profit margins.
* Analyze customer segments and monthly sales trends.
* Examine order status, delivery time, and shipping costs.
* Recommend strategies to improve business performance.

## Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas and NumPy
* Matplotlib and Seaborn
* MySQL
* Jupyter Notebook

## Key Performance Indicators

* **Total Revenue:** 11,370,043.99
* **Total Profit:** 2,215,916.27
* **Average Amount per Record:** 379.00
* **Overall Profit Margin:** 19.49%

## Key Findings

* Electronics generated the highest total revenue and total profit.
* Beauty recorded the highest profit margin among the analyzed product categories.
* Fashion also showed a relatively high profit margin.
* The lowest discount group (0–10%) had a higher profit margin than the higher discount groups.
* Groceries had the highest record count and the lowest average profit per record among the product categories.

These findings describe patterns in the dataset and do not necessarily establish cause and effect.

## Business Recommendations

1. Review discount strategies to balance sales volume and profitability.
2. Investigate why Electronics has a lower margin despite generating the highest total profit.
3. Explore opportunities to maintain the strong margins seen in Beauty and Fashion.
4. Review the pricing, costs, and product mix of Groceries to improve average profit.
5. Monitor revenue, profit margins, delivery performance, and customer ratings over time.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Python and MySQL can be used to analyze e-commerce data, identify profitability patterns, and develop practical business recommendations using data-driven evidence.
